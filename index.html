How to play

  1. 👆Drag anywhere below the middle, or on the rail under the court, to move your paddle.
  2. ↝Flick sideways as the ball lands to curve it. The computer cannot read a curve.
  3. ✹Supers: returns fill your meter. When it glows, flick as you hit to fire it.
  4. ◐Hit off-center to angle your return. Every hit speeds the ball up.
  5. ✦Grab power-ups with the ball: Wide, Shrink, Turbo, Ghost and Multiball. Whoever hit last gets it.
