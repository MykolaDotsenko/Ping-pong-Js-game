How to play

  1. 👆Drag anywhere below the middle, or on the rail under the court, to move your paddle.
  2. ↝Flick sideways as the ball lands to put a curve on it. The computer cannot read a curve.
  3. ◐Hit off-center to angle your return. Every hit speeds the ball up.
  4. ✦Grab power-ups with the ball: Wide, Shrink, Turbo and Ghost. Whoever hit last gets it.

Small product, explicit boundaries

A neon arcade Pong with no framework, no game engine and no runtime dependency, so every engineering decision stays visible.

  • Domain Scoring, collisions, spin, power-ups and the computer opponent. Deterministic and browser-free.
  • Application The match state machine, a fixed-timestep loop with slow motion and hit-stop, and events that drive sound, music, vibration and effects.
  • Adapters Canvas rendering, Web Audio, multi-touch and keyboard input, and the interface around the board.
